---
title: "I told my AI to procrastinate"
date: 2026-11-19
---

Something funny happened today. I added procrastination to my AI’s skills. On purpose.

Yesterday I watched [an interview with Rory Sutherland](https://youtu.be/Wi-0UUacBgQ?t=1480), the advertising man. It reminded me of something I’ve known since I watched [John Cleese’s talk on creativity](https://youtu.be/Pb5oIIPO62g?t=1220): not jumping to a decision too fast is a form of creativity. When you decide too fast, you give up on better ideas. That’s why a lot of creative people procrastinate.

I noticed my design assistant didn’t. When it looked for examples or references, it took whatever came up first and moved on.

So now, when it has a list of references, it has to stop and ask: is this enough? Does this actually satisfy the goal I’m trying to reach?

This works for anything creative you do with AI, whether it’s bots, agents or assistants. The fast answer is rarely the best one. Sometimes the most useful thing you can tell your AI is: not yet.
