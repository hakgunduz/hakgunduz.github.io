---
title: "Let yourself be ambitious"
date: 2026-11-07
---

Let yourself be ambitious. Give yourself permission to have a vision and take it seriously.

Keep saying no until you’re satisfied with what you’ve made. If something doesn’t feel right to you, say so. You don’t have to settle just because you’ve already spent time on it.

Don’t be afraid of disappointing others along the way. You need to be able to say, “This is what I want,” and keep working toward it, even when someone else thinks it’s good enough.
