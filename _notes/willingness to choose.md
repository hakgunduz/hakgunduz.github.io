---
title: "Willingness to choose"
date: 2026-11-07
---

Willpower can be understood as the ability to make decisions and take responsibility for their consequences. Choosing something also means accepting responsibility for what follows.

Owning those consequences requires self-assurance: trusting your judgment and your ability to deal with whatever comes next. The outcome is uncertain, but you can still trust yourself to figure things out.

The connection between willpower and self-assurance lies in that willingness to choose, accept the consequences, and handle them without leaving those decisions to others.
