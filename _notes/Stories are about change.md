From Will Storr's interview with David Perell: [Learn Storytelling in 72 Minutes](https://youtu.be/kDrsYMW_EYg)

- Stories are about change. That's a big thing.
- The five-act structure built around change is amazing with the examples, especially The Godfather.
- Everything falls under three areas: survival, connection and status. Popular stories touch all three.
- A theory of control, and causality.
