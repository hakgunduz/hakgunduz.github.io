It’s written by **Noah Hawley** based on the film by **Joel** and **Ethan Coen.**

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/1*cviYE9bpEGtm4nbKNTnF_Q.jpeg)

**First scene: LORNE MALVO** is sitting behind the wheel. As he drives a THUMPING comes from inside the trunk. A DEER appears on the road and MALVO HITS it. The CAR SKIDS off the road, ROLLS twice — the trunk popping open — before coming to rest on its wheels.

A MAN emerges from the trunk and LOPES off through the snow. The driver’s door opens. Malvo climbs out. He could catch him if he tried but he makes no attempt. Instead he walks to the deer, stands over the wounded animal, looking down.

The scene tells everything we need to know about him. He’s a criminal or a murderer of some kind because he kidnapped a person. And he’s a calm type which gives him a subtle craziness. He doesn’t run after the man, instead goes over to deer and stares at it. **What does he see in there?**

There are a lot of questions like this one in the script. It’s making sure that you’re wondering the same things as script suggested.

And the character descriptions:

**LORNE MALVO, age unknown, birthplace unknown.**

**LESTER NYGAARD. He’s the kind of guy who apologizes when you step on his foot.**

Not exactly drawing a picture on reader’s mind but you know these characters instantly. You get a sense of which kind of people they are.

**Next scene: LESTER NYGAARD.** His wife is talking to him nonstop. And he hears the surging, churning sound of the washing machine. You get a sense that it’s not working well. And it’s not only the washing machine. You immediately feel that there are a lot of broken things in his life.

The bad guy, Lorne Malvo doesn’t act as antagonistic force any time now. Lester’s life is circled around by his wife, his work, his brother, his washing machine. They all act as antagonistic forces, pushing him to make decisions.

Next scene, Lester tries to sell insurance. He tries to make ‘his own winnings’ like his brother but life seem to fall down on him all the time. Because his customer turns out to be librarian.

Next scene, while Lester staring in through the plate glass window at a brand new washer dryer set, he comes upon SAM HESS. A typical bully. And his sons are as big as him. Sam bullies good old Lester the pencil dick again and FAKES A PUNCH. Lester panics, turns and RUNS FACE FIRST into the PLATE GLASS WINDOW of the store. CRACK! He falls to the ground.

He didn’t have to fake a punch. They could’ve written it as a typical bully scene but instead, they gave it a twist. Sam fakes a punch and Lester hits his face by himself.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/1*JaDvR3uUEPLQfxecDAmWXA.jpeg)

In the next scene, Lorne Malvo and Lester meets in the waiting room at the hospital. The dialogue between them reveals major things about Malvo’s character. He’s a kind of guy who guides people to do bad things. But meeting with Lester’s helplessness a new circumstance opens. Lester involuntarily asks Malvo to kill Sam Hess.

Then comes the Police Officers, VERN and MOLLY. They’re at the site of Malvo’s incident. And they find the deer in the trunk. Still alive. Insight about Malvo’s character. He has no sympathy toward anything at all.

Vern goes to his home, we see that his wife is pregnant. And they talk about kid’s room color. Vern is a good man. Script plays well with the character traits. Gives you hints about the characters and which kind of people they are.

Malvo is at Sam Hess’ Trucking Company. He confronts Sam and his hired muscle. Not intimidated. He just gives Sam a slow once over. Then leaves. What a character.

Lester is at his brother’s house eating dinner. His brother, RON is exactly the opposite of him. He’s successful, he fixes things and he has a machine gun. Lester’s wife keeps complaining that he can’t be like his little brother.

Malvo talks on the phone while following Sam Hess. Revelations: he’s a gun for hire and he says that he took a detour. He’s doing this Sam Hess thing personally. Not an assignment.

Then in the next scene, he kills Hess.

Police Officers, Vern and Molly come to see the crime scene. They talk about a syndicate of fellas outta Fargo.

Malvo checks in to a motel. Meeting a TEEN there. Owner lady yells at the teen and then, Malvo finds him and convinces the teen to piss on lady’s car. Then, he snitches on the teen. He just likes to mess around with people.

Vern and Molly go over Hess’ house to talk to the wife.

Malvo calls there asking for the big boy, Mickey. Tells him that his father left all of his money to his little one, Moe. Mickey gets upset and then, angry. He goes over to Moe and beats him on the background while Police and Mrs. Hess talking.

Lester sees Malvo’s motel room. And then, he hears about Sam Hess and that he’s dead.

While Malvo is eating, Lester comes at him asking about Hess. But Malvo accuses Lester of murdering. Because he wanted him to kill Hess. And he says to Lester that he’s more man now than the day before.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:1400/1*H7gTYBp6Vl-k7ooozLlz6A.jpeg)

Molly goes to the hospital and talks to the Nurse. She mentions Lester and some guy with a head injury. And she says that they were talking about Sam Hess the dead fella. Molly lets her chief Vern know about this.

Lester now is in the basement trying to fix the darn washing machine feeling more alive and more man now. His wife comes at him. They start the engine but it quickly dies. This time for sure. She starts yelling at him, accusing him that he’s not a man. He’s not a half man.

BUT LESTER HAD ENOUGH of this. He looks at his tools. ANGLE ON THE CLAW HAMMER.

He kills his wife. Beats her to death.

Then he comes up with a plan. He calls Malvo. Then, he brings up the SHOTGUN.

Clarity is everything for this script. It explains that Lester is going to frame Malvo of his wife’s murder.

But of course, nothing goes as planned. He hears a knock on the door. Chief of Police, Vern is there. He catches Lester off guard.

Malvo comes with Lester’s SHOTGUN. Killing the chief. Then, asks for shells for the SHOTGUN. Lester says he doesn’t have any. Trying his chances about the plan of framing Malvo again. But the Police Officer, Molly arrives at the house.

Malvo vanishes from the basement, leaving Lester trapped in this situation.

He sees the inspirational poster. There’s always one, be the one.

He runs towards the wall — knocking himself out.

Then, Molly comes to the house. Draws her gun and comes in. Again, script explains: “We know Malvo could still be in the house. Each blind corner and dark space offers certain death.”

Next scene, another character intro. GUS, a traffic police. He catches Malvo on high speed and about to check his ID but Malvo gets away by intimidating and threatening the officer. Gus backs away, scared.

Lester is in the hospital. **Could he have gotten away with it?**

Then he sees the BANDAGE on his right hand. There’s a shotgun pallet in his hand. They didn’t notice it yet?

**PILOT ENDS WITH**: MOLLY — his friend and mentor died. GUS saw Malvo and never can forget about him now. LESTER, he’s a killer now and there’s a shotgun pallet in his hand. What is he going to do? MALVO is wanted by the police.

A lot had happened and we’re already wondering what happens next to these characters.
