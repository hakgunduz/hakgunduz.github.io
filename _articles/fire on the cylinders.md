I came across to Reddit the other day and thought it would be nice to have it on my Medium feed. Being bold and prepared, I think not only applies to comedy writers but to everything in life.

write every day

*“If you write every day, over the months you stockpile funny ideas, jokes, and characters that you can spin into sketches. This way, when an opportunity like this comes along, you’re confidently flipping through a pile of finished writing looking for “selects,” as opposed to trying to write several hilarious sketches at the last minute, which is really hard. This puts you in a much better position.”*

fire on all cylinders

*“The application asks for “Bio / Work.” This field is optional, but I can assure you, they’re looking carefully at this. They want to know you’ve also been doing stand-up, taking comedy classes, making youtube videos, writing articles, and writing jokes. Any comedy experience or accolades you’ve racked up need to be here stated briefly.”*
