I grew up in a remote valley in western Anatolia, always with a pencil and a piece of paper in hand, drawing and writing about everything that interested me: things I envied, or simply wanted to copy. That was how I looked at the world, and how I made sense of my feelings and of life. I still have the clipboard I used as a kid, and many, many, many notebooks have been filled since.

Now I live in Istanbul, work as a marketer and write whenever I can: fiction and non-fiction, essays, articles, notes, journals, whatever needs writing down. I doodle, draw and sketch too, because I tend to see things as shapes, narratives and structures. I watch people closely. The small mechanics of human behavior interest me, and so do my own, especially the way my mind works. Every so often something grabs me so completely that time stretches out, and I’m hooked on that feeling. This site is an imaginary room in my personal library, where all of that writing ends up. It’s a playground for ideas. I’d like it to be a small corner of the internet with a person in it.
