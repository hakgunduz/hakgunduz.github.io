I grew up in a remote valley in western Anatolia, where ancient men once held court. Today I work as a marketer and write whenever I can: fiction and non-fiction, essays, articles, notes, journals, whatever needs writing down. I doodle, draw and sketch too, because I tend to see things as shapes, narratives and structures. I watch people closely. The small mechanics of human behavior interest me, and so do my own, especially the way my mind works. Every so often something grabs me so completely that time stretches out, and I’m hooked on that feeling. This site is an imaginary room in my personal library, where all of that writing ends up. It’s a playground for ideas. I’d like it to be a small corner of the internet with a person in it.
