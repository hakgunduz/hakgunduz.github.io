They had planned a Sunday activity to replace the plastic pots of the ten newly bought plants with ceramic pots.

“Isn't the color match of the ceramics very good?” said Oya excitedly. She was putting the ceramic pots in her hand down one by one.

“Yes, this workshop makes beautiful flower pots,” said Tuğçe. She also placed the plants in her hands on the ground. They had just entered the house.

There was not much furniture in the house. The existing ones were also not placed in an orderly manner. It was obvious that Oya had just moved into the house. She came to the window.

“I think I will put the plants here,” she said.

Tuğçe looked at the sun-drenched window. She thought that it would not be compatible with plants, but she did not say anything so as not to upset Oya.

"Maybe you don't want to put them all in the same place?" she could say though.

“No, I want them all together. I want it to be like a plant garden. I will spend time here,” said Oya.

“Don't worry too much about the furniture, my parents sent them. Old furniture. I hope to get new ones in time. Now that I think about it, the colors of the pots don't quite match the furniture.”

All of a sudden, Oya seemed frustrated. Tuğçe put the last plant she had on the ground. “Where and how shall we do it? Shall we start now?"

“Sure, I arranged the newspaper. We can do it here. We put the newspapers,” said Oya. She stood in the middle of the large hall.

“Actually, let's not bother with laying out newspapers. The dirt will spread everywhere anyway. Let's pull that carpet, let's do it on the floor. It will be more comfortable if we sweep it last," said Tuğçe.

“But…” Oya was able to say. She was upset that the plan she had in mind was not followed. “Without newspapers, we get dirty.”

“We will get dirty anyway. I dressed accordingly,” Tuğçe said with a smile.

Oya didn't find it very funny. "Then I'll change myself," she said.

Tuğçe had already started to move out the carpet. Then she set the plants aside and the ceramic pots aside. She came from the kitchen with a knife and pierced the bag of soil they had brought. Some of the soil was spilled on the floor.

When Oya returned to the hall, Tuğçe was spilling the soil on the ground.

"What are you doing!?" said Oya.

“It will be easier that way. Come, let me show you how," said Tuğçe. However, something struck her mind.

“If only there was something to transfer the soil to the pot…” she said and looked left and right. She saw the toy box on the shelf. Inside was a plastic shovel.

“I bought that set because my nephew might play with the soil. But he never likes to get dirty. Same as me,” said Oya.

Tugçe put the shovel beside her and sat down on the ground again. She knocked it out from under the plastic pot of the snake plant she took in her hand. She crushed the soil with her hand and freed the roots from the soil. On the one hand, she was telling Oya about the steps she took.

Together they began to move the plants into their new pots by emptying the pots and filling the ceramic pots with soil.

After a while, Oya asked Tuğçe, "What would you do if a thief broke into your house, have you ever thought about it?" asked.

“A thief? No, I don't think so," Tuğçe said.

“I always think about it,” said Oya. “I've been scared ever since I moved here. What if I wake up one morning and meet a thief?”

“Did you change the door lock when you moved?” ' Tuğçe asked.

“Yes,” said Oya. “Actually, there is a camera at the door.”

“Why are you afraid if there is a camera?” Tuğçe was surprised.

"I don't know. It always comes to my mind. Surely there are ways to turn off the camera,” said Oya as she tried to clear the growing branches of the ivy from the soil.

“The pothos in your hand will bring positive energy to the house. Don't worry," Tuğçe said. "It's very easy to look after, and it doesn't need a lot of light."

“I'll take one of these to my grandfather,” said Oya. “Since I lost my grandmother, my mind has always been on my grandfather. I am very afraid that something will happen to him.”

“Is he sick now? What's wrong?"

“He's okay, he's not sick. But he is old. We are afraid,” said Oya.

“Ivy is very easy to propagate. I'll show you. You'll have lots of vines. You can take one of them to your grandfather," she said.

“Look, this is the Zamia plant,” Tuğçe continued. “It doesn't need a lot of light, even if you water it irregularly, you won't have much trouble. If you forget, nothing happens. I think it suits you perfectly.”

“No, I just water them every two or three days.” ' Oya muttered. She was admiring the spider plant in her hand. “This is so beautiful, I love it.”

“I invited someone from work to the theater. I hope he didn't misunderstand,” she continued.

“What will he misunderstand?” ' Tuğçe asked.

“I don't know, maybe he thought I had a different purpose. He's very good at conversation, but frankly, I don't have much other interest. I didn't want to go alone, so when you said you weren't available, I called him. I hope he doesn't think I'm interested."

“Yeah, it's a little weird. But anyway, if he thinks, let him think. Let him think he's on a date with you and be happy,” Tuğçe said with a smile.

“What if it gets heard at work? What do people think? Do you think this will affect my relationships at work?” She was trying to get the plant in her hand out of its plastic pot.

“Why should you, my dear?” Tuğçe was surprised.

“I don't know, sometimes some companies have such weird policies. They can't say it openly. But they're firing people and stuff. I just moved in. I'm not ready for something like this right now. What if I don't go?"

“Don't go if you don't want to,” said Tuğçe.

“Then, I would be known as a problematic person. What a shame if I invite someone to the theater and don't go myself," said Oya. “It doesn't come out of the pot. I will try to cut it. There would be a utility knife around here.”

She found the utility knife and came back to sit on the ground. She picked up the pot and tried to cut it, but on the first move, the blade slipped from her hand and cut her finger.

"Watch out!" Tuğçe ran to Oya. She looked at her finger. She saw that it was a very small cut.

“It was a small cut, fortunately,” she said. She picked up the utility knife. It looked brand new. There was no mold on it.

“Let's go to the hospital,” said Oya.

“No dear, what hospital? A tiny cut,” said Tuğçe, and went inside.

“What if it gets infected?”

Tuğçe had already returned with alcohol, a band-aid, and a napkin. She deftly pressed Oya's finger to the alcohol. She then wrapped her finger tightly with a tissue and a band-aid.

“You are okay,” Tuğçe said.

Tuğçe's vibe of knowing what she was doing calmed Oya a little. Slowly, she began to return to her work.

“I haven't exercised for several weeks,” said Oya. She was trying to pull the lily out of its pot with one hand.

“How will our legs carry us when we are old? We have to start exercising now.”

“I read an article once: Exercising is not about preventing health problems or punishing your body, but celebrating what your body can do. I was very impressed,” said Tuğçe.

"It's a bit unreasonable, what are we celebrating?" said Oya. “Do you think the boy I invited to the theater does sports?”

“I don't know, I've never seen the boy. You know. Was his body fit?” said Tuğçe.

“He has been very motivated since the first day he started the job. He's moving up very fast,” said Oya. “I have been staying in the same place since I started working here. What I should aim for, I have no idea.”

“Give yourself some time. You'll find something in the end," said Tuğçe. “You already have a very good job. You don't have to worry."

“Yes, but we have to think about the future,” said Oya. “What if the landlord says I should leave tomorrow? Have you ever seen the new leases? Rents skyrocketed a month after I moved in here.”

“You just got in the house, why would he want you out?”

“I don't know, you never know with the landlords. I always think about it,” said Oya.

She was trying to get the big monstera they bought out of its pot. It came off easily after hitting the sole a few times. She wanted to choose the most beautiful one among the ceramic pots.

“I stopped reading books because of dealing with all these troubles. I am not doing anything to improve myself,” complained Oya.

She caught sight of a light pink and slightly larger flower pot. She filled it with soil and placed the monstera.

“I don't do any creative work either, I've become dull.”

Tuğçe was listening to her while shaking the plant in her hand.

“I feel lagging when talking to people because I couldn't improve myself,” said Oya. “I act as if I knew what they were talking about.”

While Oya was talking, Tuğçe potted the last plant and started grouping the plants. Oya was watching her, cleaning her hand.

“Now,” said Tuğçe. “This group doesn't want a lot of water, they don't want a lot of light either. You can water it every two to three weeks, checking the soil to see if it's dry."

“Once every two to three weeks?” ' Oya was surprised.

"Yes. This group, on the other hand, requires water every two to three days and needs to be placed in a sunny location.”

"How? Okay, let's put them all in front of the window?" said Oya.

“I was going to say something about that too. For some plants, this window front is not very good. Very sunny. They would dry up,” said Tuğçe.

She tried to take one of the flower pots and put it in front of the window. The flowerpot did not fit on the marble in front of the window. It didn't seem very balanced.

“These don't fit here,” Tuğçe said.

"What? How does it not fit!?” Oya got up and tried with another flower pot and saw that they did not fit. She was frustrated again.

“How am I going to take care of so many plants!” she got angry with Tuğçe.

"What do you mean?”

“They all need to be taken care of separately,” said Oya. “How am I going to do this? Do I have to deal with plants on top of all that?”

"Yes, but didn't you want these plants in the first place?" ' Tuğçe asked. She was very surprised.

“Yes, I wanted to, but I didn't know that they expect so much attention. Why didn't you tell me?"

“I wouldn't have guessed you'd think plants didn't want attention,” Tuğçe said. She was also starting to get angry.

“Where are we going to put all these plants now?” said Oya.

Tuğçe placed the flower pot in her hand in the middle of the dining table. Then she placed other flower pots in various parts of the house. She placed one on the shelves, the other on the small coffee table by the window, and a few on the nightstand in the bedroom.

Finally, when Tuğçe was done, Oya took a look at the house. The house looked better than she thought.

“Let's water them all,” Tuğçe said. She wanted to finish her work as soon as possible and leave the house. She regretted that she had come to help Oya.

Oya filled a jug with water and came to the hall, but she slipped by pressing the soil on the floor and spilled some of the water.

"Here you go. Now there will be mud everywhere,” she said. “I told you to lay the newspaper.”

Tuğçe parted the muddy soil with her hand. On the other hand, she was using the toy shovel he used.

Oya felt very bad for a moment because of how she treated Tuğçe. She took a deep breath. She bent down next to Tuğçe and started to help her collect the mud.

She squeezed the mud in her hand and shaped it. “It's like play dough,” she said.

"Isn't it?"

“How does my nephew not like playing with mud?” said Oya. “Look, it's so much fun.”

She made a small mud cake from the mud in her hand. She put one of the fallen leaves on it.

Tuğçe started to play with mud also. She was making various shapes with her hands. Oya got up and took the toys from the shelf.

They sat together and started playing with the mud. They had decided to build a castle. In the middle of the hall, the sun was shining in patches and the wind from the window made the curtain dance.

Tuğçe decided to stay a little longer.
