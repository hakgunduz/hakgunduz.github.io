---
title: "Expeditions of Scaredy Tom, Part 2"
date: 2023-05-07 19:55:46 +0000
---

Before entering the forest, Tom glanced back at the wooden walls surrounding their town. Big cuts and marks on the walls caught his attention. There were many. It seemed strange to him. He couldn’t understand what might have caused it.

He took in the view of the town. It was standing still right at the center of the small clearing. The branches danced, making the patch of sky above town seem like it could move away any second.

Tom took another look at the clouds and then grabbed his bag. He headed for the forest. The map he was holding was old and battered. The circles marked on the map started close by and then continued until they were out of the map.

They followed a trail. The first one was nearby to a river. Tom couldn’t tell if the roots were coming out of the river or if they were heading for it. He took out his notebook and a pen.

Until he could finish describing the circle, the day was almost over. Tom looked for a place to sleep and found it outside the circle. There was clear grass below it. He camped there. His travel bag was full of food but he didn’t know what else to bring. So, he didn’t have anything that would make him comfortable sleeping there.

He turned and turned until he started hearing sounds coming from deep into the forest. “It is the wind,” he thought but he had his back on the tree trunk nevertheless.

It was not just the wind, there were bug sounds too. Each minute he couldn’t sleep he started hearing different sounds until suddenly it was morning.

“It’s time to inspect the second one,” he thought and found the circle easily by following the map. And to his surprise spotting the orb was also easy. It glowed patch brown. But once Tom moved to grab it, the orb disappeared.

He looked around to see where it went and saw it on the other side of the circle. “It teleported,” he thought. As he tried to grab it, the orb kept teleporting.

Tom was exhausted. Running around the teleporting orb added to the lack of sleep. He climbed outside to lie down on the high ground facing the circle. After a few seconds, he saw movement on the opposite side.

The reflection of a green glow flickered behind the roots. Tom thought of the tribeless girl. She spawned on the root. Then, she slid down to run around dancing. She kept climbing on each root and sliding down.

She was having fun. It was also fun to watch for Tom. But then, he noticed. The roots started to shudder. The one underneath her feet started moving and she fell. It carried her to the patch brown orb.

Tom gasped. The girl heard it but didn’t see where it came from. She took the orb and with it, she started hovering on top of the roots. The brown glow helped her zoom around the circle for a while until she spotted Tom. Then, she stopped.

She dropped the orb to the ground. Tom ran to grab it but the orb was devoured.

“Why did you drop it?” Tom asked.

“It’s not mine to take,” she said and hopped around heading out.

Tom tried to keep up with her but she was fast. Although to his surprise, she was at the next circle examining the three glowing buttons on the tree trunk.

“Did they also give you the task of the expedition?” Tom asked.

The tribeless girl was puzzled, first to see him there and second to the question. She shrugged off the first part and then answered.

“What task?”

“Why are you going on about the undiscovered circles then?”

“Undiscovered?” She repeated but it seemed she didn’t hear what Tom said. “It’s fascinating,” she continued. Then, pushed on one of the glowing buttons on the tree trunk.

They heard a rumbling and a cloud of dust covered the circle. After the dust was settled they realized the circle rearranged itself.

“How did you do it?” Tom asked.

“I didn’t do anything,” she answered.

“Why are you taking this lightly? Circles are a serious matter,” he said.

“Are they?” she said and pushed another button.

With a rumbling and a cloud of dust, the circle rearranged itself. Again.

“Stop it,” Tom demanded and guarded himself between her and the tree. “We should find a way to extract the orb. Stop, playing with it.”

“You and your extractions,” she said and without even Tom could make a move, she slid around to push the third button.

A rumbling and a cloud of dust, again.

“It’s a riddle. It’s fun,” she said.

“You are going to ruin it again,” he said. “We can’t extract it by toying with it. Do you even know where the orb is?”

“Maybe there is no orb,” she answered.

He glanced around. “This is a circle,” he said.

“And?” she asked.

“And what? Circles have orbs.”

“So far, this one hasn’t,” she said.

Tom took out his notepad and pen and started taking notes. The tribeless girl was examining each root.

“What did you mean by expedition task?” she asked.

“I’ve been sent to gather information about undiscovered circles,” he said, then realized he was puffing up his chest. “Why are you here?”

“One of my older friends asked me what kind of person I want to be when I grow up,” she said.

Tom dropped his pen and grabbed it back before she noticed. The roots around her started to shudder again.

“I told them I don’t have an answer yet,” she continued not realizing the movement of the roots. “So, I got here to explore, to discover more.”

She walked around with the roots dancing behind her.

“Someone once told me, the best way to unravel one's own is to follow the path of the circles.”

After taking a turn behind the last root, a soft pink orb revealed itself to her. But this time, Tom wanted to take matters into his own hands. He was ready to rush to the orb once it revealed itself.

However, with his first step forward, he heard a rumbling. A cloud of dust covered his vision. Once he could see, there was neither orb nor the tribeless girl. Tom ran around the roots to find her.

“Where are you girl?” he shouted.

He rushed back to the glowing buttons, pushed each, and examined the rearranged circle. With each push, the circle rearranged in a new way. But there was no sign of the tribeless girl.

“Where did you go?” he asked.

“I’m here,” her voice said from behind the roots.

“And my name is Enya,” the voice continued.
