---
title: "Lost Remnants of My Life"
date: 2023-03-05 15:12:38 +0000
---

“Grandpa Sami, if you had a time machine and could go back to your memories, what are the things you would collect from your life and bring them today?” my granddaughter asked me.

Aylin was sitting in a corner playing a game with herself. I never expected that she would ask me a question at that moment. I never expected that she would ask such a deep question.

"I don't know," was all I could say.

“Come on, grandpa, think about it,” Aylin insisted.

Many aspects of my life passed before my eyes. Some of these were memories that I had never shared with my family. Before I met my ex-wife who was Aylin's grandmother, I hadn't told many people about my experiences.

The first thing that came to my mind was the shoe company I once tried to start. But I didn't want to share it with Aylin at that moment because it ended in failure.

"What are you doing there, let me see?" I asked. I was hoping the subject would change. In one corner of the patio, she was playing with the soil she had poured out of a plant pot, a few wooden kitchen spoons, and many other things.

“I saw a friend of mine recently, he had a desk like this.” She was trying to explain the shape of the table to me with her hands in an overly excited way. As far as I could tell, the table was her own size.

“There was a lot of stuff in it. There were small shovels. There were different containers on the table where you sorted things out. Grandpa, it was beautiful! You could do whatever you wanted,” I didn't quite understand what kind of table he was talking about, but I could read the excitement in her eyes.

When my daughter Nurten came to pick up Aylin, I asked her. “The sensory gaming table,” she said. “She saw it at her friend’s place. She cannot stop talking about it since then. We looked at the prices, it is very expensive.”

I was very surprised when I asked how much the table cost. It seemed strange that a toy table was so expensive. I wouldn't have enough money either. When I was my granddaughter's age, I thought I could buy all the toys I wanted when I got older.

After I took my daughter, Aylin, I cleaned up the veranda a bit. If there is no work in the garden, I usually spend time here. That question was on my mind.

I remembered my excitement about being a shoe manufacturer. We had set up the company with big dreams. We found very good employees. I became good friends with all of them. I was so sure things would go well.

I never told anyone about it in my later life. I have never told this story to anyone. I disappointed those nice employees, good-hearted, hardworking people. And for what? Because I didn’t know what I was doing...

What was my purpose? What was I aiming for? I had no idea what I was doing. We tried to establish the company with enthusiasm. Why shoes, I never knew. All I knew was the idea that I'd been trying to buy better shoes all my life.

I always wanted, I was always looking for better ones. But they were so expensive that I couldn't afford them. I thought that one day my luck would turn and I could easily buy all those shoes. My luck never turned. Buying whatever shoe I want has always remained a dream.

At that time, I used to wear cheap shoes. I had no choice. They were not suitable for my aesthetic tastes. Maybe that's why I decided to start a shoe company.

But at the end of the day, what we produced did not give me what I wanted. I was ashamed to wear it. I was stressed, imagining questions people might ask.

Why did Aylin ask me such a question out of the blue? How could I answer?

After the shoe company went bankrupt, I did various jobs. Most of them did not make me happy at all. I either quit my job or was fired after short periods of time.

The longest job I did at that time was logistics in a company. I was just driving. Goods had to be constantly transported from one place to another. I had no idea what they were doing. It was a job I took just because I knew how to drive.

The thought that spending months and years just behind the wheel was not something I wanted to tell Aylin.

I also met my daughter's mother during this period. I met when I was trying to build a shoe company, and since it went downhill for me after that, our relationship could not progress very well.

That's what kept me going in the logistics business for so long.

In the second year of our relationship, we got married and had children right away. Now that we have a child, my wife asked me to keep a regular job. At that time, I was able to find a logistics job. However, after two years of fighting and arguments, we decided to break up.

Then I quit my job. I was of no use. I spent that period thinking about how little I contributed to life. When did I ever do anything?

In my life where I was dragged aimlessly, on the one hand, I was trying to understand why I was holding on to the things I was holding on to, on the other hand, I was getting bored of them pretty quickly and changing my mind.

I hope Aylin doesn't keep asking me such questions. I hope she's not like me. I hope she becomes a successful, self-confident person like her mother.

In the next period, after doing various jobs again, we decided to start a new company with someone I met. We wanted to create a brand that meets basic clothing needs. Underwear, t-shirt, tank top, shorts. We only produced white and black colors.

We took care to ensure that each product is of high quality and can be used for years. However, there was something in this company that we did not think about. We produced such good products that those who bought a few t-shirts did not need white or black t-shirts for years.

We hadn't thought about how to scale the company. Again, a process started with excitement and evolved into disappointment. The company did not go bankrupt, but after years of slowly dying, we gradually went bankrupt. We had already foreseen this and started to apply for other jobs.

I was trying to remember my friendships. Many of these were relationships lost as a result of the failures I described. Of course, there were some people I still talked to from here and there, but I lost touch with many people.

I always thought that most of the people I met had achieved something one way or another. I used to dream that they landed good jobs and led happy lives and relationships. I could not be like them.

I realized that I did not tell many of these to my granddaughter Aylin, but even to my daughter Nurten. Why would they have to hear these shameful stories? I didn't have a good relationship with them anyway. They wouldn't share anything with me.

I couldn't even afford a sensory gaming table.

I've never even seen such a toy anywhere. I wonder what kind of families were the families of Aylin's friends? I'm sure they were people who lived happily and knew what they were doing.

Did Aylin think of me there when she visited another family and saw her friends' grandfather there? I couldn't even imagine what kind of families they were. How could I belong with them?

I hope one day she won't ask me to do something like that.

Why was the question he asked me so interesting? What does it mean to collect memories and bring them to the present? I was wondering if it should be small items that I can carry, memories.

When setting up the shoe company, we met with the first team we hired. It was one of the best moments of my life. We did a sketch of a shoe together. I wish I could take a copy of that draft with me.

I remember now, we weren't upset at all when the company went bankrupt. We had all kept in touch for years and kept each other informed about our lives.

However, over time, these turned into a cause of anxiety for me because I felt compelled to report current news about my life every time, and I remembered that there were no exciting things in my life at all.

How I pushed these people away over time… I wish I had a copy of one of those designs right now.

In fact, there were some of my company founder friends whom I met at that time and still talked to. Every time they tried to contact me, I got angry and didn't answer.

I thought about what I could bring from the logistics business to today. Of course, I would love to be able to take my miniature cup keychain with me. I remember the day they gave it to me. The atmosphere inside… Actually, we were like a family.

I loved everyone I worked with at that job. There was a knit piece on the key chain. The founder of the company started the first business with the same car I drove. That knit piece was very special for him. Actually, I thought about that first but I couldn't take it with me.

As much as I love my key chain, I also loved the ceramic cup that I used every day. They gave me a miniature version of the same cup to put on my key chain.

I carried it with me for years. But one day I got angry to see the keychain and threw it somewhere in the house and then I couldn't find it at all.

I would like to take customer letters with me from our other business. The buyers of our products sent such heartfelt letters that it was the reason why we tried not to shut down the company but to continue it gradually over the years.

We even received hundreds of letters after we closed the company. They wanted us to continue production. But they all already had our products. They would not take more.

I wish I could take a picture of my ex-wife. I would like a photo of her from our last fight that caused us to break up. More than anything. She was so beautiful. At that moment, I wanted to take back everything so much.

I wish I could have a photo of her from that day, even if nothing has changed in my life. Even if everything ends the same, I would like to appreciate the time we spent together more.

Then it suddenly occurred to me. I never understood what this event meant, which I experienced a few days after I broke up with my ex-wife.

I saw the rose in a shoe polisher's bag while walking on the sidewalk. He wanted to shine my shoes when I asked him what it meant. He was a young man. Out of curiosity, I sat down and let her shine my shoes. I was going to give him my last money that day.

He began to tell me that he was traveling the world with a bag on his back. He was on his way to the next city, earning enough money through shining shoes wherever he went.

That day he gave me tips on how to take better care of my shoes. He looked at my shoes, which I did not like and was ashamed to wear, and wished me well to wear them for years.

“Short-term lifestyles start every day to suffocate us,” he said.

I wish I had asked him to write down his tips on a piece of paper. I didn't really do what he said, but I wouldn't want to forget the memory of that moment.

I was very happy to remember that moment again.

Immediately after, I called my son-in-law. He was very surprised that I had called him since we hadn't spoken much in a long time. He didn't know how to answer the phone. I had already conveyed my request without giving him the right to speak.

“Just find a template online for the sensory toy table and fetch it. Let's do it ourselves," I said.

I ran to the warehouse and took out the wooden pieces. I started to sort out the ones that could be useful for us and the ones that wouldn't work for us and put them in a corner.

A few hours later, my son-in-law and Aylin arrived together.

“We will make our own table!” I said to Aylin.

“I brought a lot of paint. We will paint it the color we want!” she said, bouncing with excitement.

After we started making the table, I told Aylin that I would answer her question.

And I began to tell her about the lost remnants of my life…
