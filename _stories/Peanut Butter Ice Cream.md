---
title: "Peanut Butter Ice Cream"
date: 2023-01-08 18:25:20 +0000
---

When Tarik saw her for the first time, he got hit by a volleyball in the face and had to leave the field with blood on his nose.

The summer camp was a camp that lasted for two months and included various activities in its program. It was a tradition for students to relax before they moved on to their senior year of high school.

High schools from various districts participated in the tournaments. Tarık also was in the volleyball team that summer, with the force of his teacher. He was prominent in physical activities. But he was not very good at communication. Although he was invited to football, basketball, and table tennis teams, he did not prefer to join the teams of these sports. His volleyball coach Fuat was one of the few people he respected and could easily get along with with his limited communication skills. That's why he always felt indebted to him.

After the first two wins, Zerrin slipped into the audience in the middle of the third match, which was not going great. She rushed to join a group of people and began to tell them something.

The first detail that caught Tarık's attention was that Zerrin could apply moisturizer to her lips while talking. This small detail was the main reason why he didn't notice the volleyball coming toward his face.

“I am okay, I am okay,” he said to his teammates, who were crowding around him, and retreated into a corner. While the cleaning staff was cleaning the blood on the floor, Tarık's eyes were looking for Zerrin again. Coach quickly grasped these developments and removed Tarık from the game. With his bleeding nose, it seemed unlikely that he could continue.

They lost the match when he was out of the game, but Tarik was not even aware of what had happened. At the end of the match, he realized that the group including Zerrin had come to their side. They discussed what to do after the match. The team players quickly went to take a shower.

Tarik never spent time with his teammates. Even though some of the team went out in the evenings, he would stay in his bunk and read a book. However, that day, he went after his teammates with the thought of seeing Zerrin a little more.

They were walking on the beach in a large group. Various hotels and cafes were lined up along the beach. Zerrin continued to walk with great passion, involved in a heated dialogue. She was not looking ahead, but rather at her friends who were listening to her. That's why Tarık was able to follow Zerrin throughout the entire walk, as she almost walked backward. They even made eye contact a few times.

Tarık was walking in the back with his less social teammates. Even in their presence, he felt excluded.

Suddenly, voices arose among the group's front walkers, and attention was turned to a karaoke event in the garden of a hotel. Zerrin was excitedly trying to persuade the people next to her to join the karaoke.

A few people from the group, led by Zerrin, went directly to the person sitting at a table next to the stage. They signed up. The rest of the group settled into empty chairs in the audience located in the hotel's large courtyard.

Tarık was so frightened by the idea of ​​karaoke that he couldn't even think of entering the courtyard. He was outside, alone. The left side of the road was looking at the courtyard of the hotel, and the beach was on the right. He sat on one of the piles of sunbeds here and watched Zerrin with his back to the sea.

The sounds coming from the hotel courtyard were intertwined with the sound of the waves crashing at the back, and it was as if they all formed the melodies of a musical starring Zerrin.

He was never in the leading group. He had never seen the courage to appear on the karaoke stage in himself. He could never draw all the attention to himself with a loud voice like Zerrin.

This peaceful moment was clouded by the thoughts in his mind, and he realized inwardly that he had to go. However, the idea of ​​seeing Zerrin for a few more minutes prevented him from leaving. With this dilemma inside of him, he tried to sit there all night and watch Zerrin from afar as much as he could.

When he woke up the next day, he realized how tired he was. His inner dilemma began manifesting itself physically. When he got down to breakfast he saw that the food was almost finished and there was a big queue stretched out.

The meal line continued between the tables. He walked slowly and got in line, but he was at the intersection of two tables, blocking the way for people passing by.

When the first person who wanted to pass came, he said, "Sorry," and gave way. Soon after, another person came and said, "You're blocking the way." A feeling of stress came over him as if everyone standing in the dining hall had to pass through his spot and give way to everyone. He gave up on eating, left the hotel, and started walking.

He didn't really want to have breakfast anyway. The hot weather had brought to mind the idea of ​​eating ice cream. On the first day they came to summer camp, he discovered an ice cream shop on the beach and started trying various flavors. His favorite was peanut butter ice cream. He heard it for the first time in his life, tried it for the first time, and loved it.

That morning, he went to the same ice cream shop and stood in line. The couple in front of him couldn't decide what they wanted to buy. Faced with too many options, they were evaluating their decision with serious discussion.

Tarık secretly hoped they could get the peanut butter ice cream. He wanted them to try it. He evaluated exactly where the peanut butter ice cream stood among the options. It was standing in the top row, just to the left of the rightmost strawberry, in a light brown color.

The writing was clearly handwritten by one of the employees. It was not easy to read. He thought about the readability of the “Peanut Butter”. It was not as legible as the “Strawberry” next to it.

The shop owner didn't stand right in the middle of the options. He was shifted a little to the left. He was far from the peanut butter option. Tarık thought the indecisive couple would prefer options that stood physically close to the shop owner. Indeed, what he thought came true. They had chosen a coconut and a banana option which were located right in front of the shop owner.

After the indecisive couple, he decisively ordered a scoop of coconut and a scoop of peanut butter ice cream.

“Peanut butter ice cream? Interesting!" said a voice.

When he turned, he realized that the person standing behind him was Zerrin. He didn't know what to say, he swallowed the words that came to his mind. “Yes,” was the only word that came out of his mouth. It also muffled over because it coincided with Zerrin's moment of ordering peanut butter ice cream.

"Weren't you walking with us last night?" said Zerrin. “I saw a ball hit your nose on the field, there was blood everywhere.”

Without waiting for an answer, she studied Tarık's face.

“You look quite alright,” he said. “You suddenly disappeared last night.”

For a moment, it was very surprising to Tarık that Zerrin noticed a person like himself among all the things. He began to doubt the reality of the moment he was living in.

“Yeah, I was a little tired,” he was able to say.

"Good good. Rest. Get a good rest. You are playing with our team in a few days. Beat them okay? My ex plays on the team, I want him to lose and be disgraced so terribly. I think you can do it. Thank you very much,” she said and walked away with a smile.

“Peanut Butter Ice Cream!” she called back again. Then she made a sign that said, “OK!”.

Tarık didn't quite understand what he was going through. He waved, smiling.

That same evening, the volleyball team and their friends planned to go to the amusement park. Tarık thought that he would follow them eagerly and see Zerrin. However, when they entered the amusement park, he could not see Zerrin anywhere.

The air felt good to the level of caressing their skin. The distant wave crashes were intertwined with the sounds of the games inside the amusement park and danced with the machine lights flashing in various colors. Tarık realized that his senses were opening up. It was as if he was living his life for the first time.

“Shhh, peanut,” he heard a voice. “Peanut Butter!”

When he turned around, he saw Zerrin waving at him. She wore matching socks with colorful sneakers and fitted a one-piece dress celebrating the soft chill of the air. Tarık's head was spinning.

Zerrin grabbed Tarık by the arm and started dragging him, saying, “Wait until what I'm going to make you try." They went to the guy who was selling popcorn in his little cart right next to the game of shooting ducks. Zerrin pointed to the small bowl right next to the man, not the big bowl of popcorn in front of him.

They bought popcorn of a different color than regular popcorn in a newsprint cone. Zerrin watched him excitedly when Tarık threw the first popcorn into his mouth.

“It's spicy!” said Tarık.

"Yes!" Zerrin was excited. “Isn't it so good?! It's even better than the best chips I've ever had, like chips with ketchup.”

Tarık popped a few more popcorn into his mouth and thought it really was like ketchup chips.

Zerrin's attention was drawn to her friends in the bumper cars. "Come on," she pulled on Tarık and went to their friends. Despite the popcorn cone in her hand, Zerrin masterfully bought her ticket and headed to her car.

Tarık had a hard time getting his wallet out of excitement while trying to catch up with her. The worker waited for him with a small smile on his face. "I'm so sorry," said Tarık, handing him his card. When he bought his ticket and proceeded to the car, he realized that Zerrin had already gotten in the car and joined the fun with her friends.

He was amazed that even in the bumper car, she preserved her popcorn well and even continued to eat it regularly. When he got in his car and drove towards her, Zerrin immediately noticed Tarık and called him to her. She stopped right at the middle intersection of the "L" shaped track.

She told him about her plan to park both cars just like her and separate the two ends of the "L" shaped track. They were going to block the middle area.

“But people won't be able to get through,” Tarık tried to explain. However, Zerrin had already begun to yell at those who could not pass by. She looked like she was having a lot of fun.

“Guys, guys… Those who do not pay for the bridge cannot cross to the other side.” she gave a short speech. Playful “Boo” sounds were already beginning to rise among their friends.

“Thank you, thank you… In order to cross you need to promise to ride the rollercoaster with us next. But we will both sit in the front,” she said and pointed to Tarık. At that moment, Tarık's guilt of making people's lives difficult by cutting their way disappeared. The hope of spending more time with Zerrin was instrumental in opening himself up more.

He straightened up in his car and turned towards the person who constantly was trying to crash into Tarık's car. Under Zerrin's gaze, he said "You. Shall Note. Pass…” and he slapped the seat of his car. After a second of silence, everyone burst into laughter.

Other days passed by coinciding with Zerrin in various environments and spending time as if they had agreed to hang out together. They tried various local foods, played volleyball on the beach, and swam in the sea together.

However, while all this was going on, Tarık continued to be surprised that Zerrin was spending time with him. Because without addressing what their relationship had turned into, or even whether they were even friends or not, they were just hanging out by coincidence. Although Zerrin behaved as if they have been friends for years while spending time together, Tarık started to think that something was wrong or could go wrong.

He postponed all these negative thoughts until the match with her ex. Then he would open up to Zerrin and say that he liked her. That's why he had to win the match.

He spent the time he did not spend with Zerrin training with the team. After the most social week of his life, game day has arrived. He woke up nervous and began to think that it was a big day that could affect his whole life.

Until this week, it was as if his heart had never beaten in his life. He did not know how to live, he could not get himself to be motivated for anything. He felt that all this would change, even began to change.

He got off his bunk and made his way to the dining hall. One of his teammates saw him and rushed to him.

"Do you know? Today's match is canceled, and the high school that we were going to play has decided to leave the camp early. So, we technically won already. We're going to the beach soon, by the way. Grab your towel after breakfast,” he said and left.

Everything that was alive inside Tarık seemed to have turned to stone from head to toe. A gray and darkening feeling came over him.

He didn't even get Zerrin's number.

He lost his appetite, so angry at his stupidity that he thought of smashing something around him. However, he chose to walk instead. He went for a walk along the coastal path.

He first saw the amusement park and thought about the moments he spent with Zerrin. Then, he came across different places along the coast that reminded him of what they had done together with Zerrin. As he thought about the time he had spent with her for a week, his anger began to subside. The vitality he had felt before stirred awake again.

Everything started to turn colorful in his eyes again like how he saw life when he was with Zerrin. His senses were opened up, and he began to listen to the sound of the sea and street musicians. He had seen the corner where he and Zerrin used to lie on the sunbeds and read all day.

Then he examined the place where they tried eggplant pancakes together. He saw the ice cream shop just ahead. He remembered the first time he spoke to Zerrin. He went and ordered ice cream in the hope that she would appear behind him again.

He overheard a couple discussing what to get behind him as he placed his order. As Tarık was about to take his ice cream and walk away, he suddenly turned and said to the couple:

“You should definitely try the peanut butter, I recommend it,” he said.
