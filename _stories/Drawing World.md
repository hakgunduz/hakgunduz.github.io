---
title: "Drawing World"
date: 2023-04-23 19:16:43 +0000
---

"I'm sick of this shit," Jules said as he entered the nightclub. His long-distance girlfriend Naomi kept sending him messages. "She wasn't this jealous before," he thought.

He couldn't ignore his constantly vibrating phone anymore and found a less crowded spot. He sat on the empty chair at the table.

After answering Naomi's consecutive questions, he took out his notebook from his pocket. Many pages of the notebook were filled. He opened one of the last filled pages. Various plants were painted around a river with watercolors. Green and blue tones were in harmony with each other.

He took a red ink pen out of his pencil case. He started drawing different drawings on it as if he wanted to destroy the harmony on the page. He didn't pay much attention to the new drawings and even seemed to be trying to draw worse. If he didn't know that he drew the watercolor himself, he could think that the ones drawn with the red pen and the sketches drawn with the watercolor were drawn by different people.

The river represented the peaceful job he found after a long time. He was happy with the friendships he made in this new job.

Turning their moments in life into paintings and creating a drawing world was Jules' idea. When he opened this subject to Naomi, she was also excited. For a while, they both drew on their various moments in life and shared them.

For the first time in their six-year relationship, Jules experienced disappointment after these drawings. Of course, they knew each other's style until that day. However, drawing all their lives in such different styles and different approaches worried him.

He had made drawings symbolizing the business world with the happiness he felt in solving business problems that had been going on for years. Being able to exhibit his drawings was also one of the important moments in his life. He started to fill his notebooks with various drawings that symbolized these moments. In this process, Naomi drew her family, childhood friends, and her routine in the city she lived in.

They had discussed these issues in the early years of their relationship. Neither Jules nor Naomi wanted to go to the city where the other lived. Therefore, they put this issue aside and focused on their relationship. However, in recent months, Jules felt very lonely. As he felt lonely, having hundreds of kilometers between him and his girlfriend was not helpful at all.

Drawing their imaginary worlds affected Naomi also, and she started to show sides she never showed before. The sudden transformation of someone who has never been jealous for years into a jealous one surprised Jules quite a bit. However, whenever he brought up this issue, the argument heated up further with Naomi accusing Jules of not communicating as much as before.

The uncertainty he felt about his future was shaking Jules more than ever before. He had just started living alone for the first time, and he wanted Naomi to live with him at least for a while. However, at the same time, Naomi's family had bought a house for their daughter.

The night he went out to have fun with his friends had turned into hell. He had scribbled over all the watercolor drawings for hours in red pen. In the following days, he continued this new habit at every opportunity.

He didn't want to talk to Naomi like he used to. He didn't inform her of what he was doing during the day. At rare moments when they messaged each other, both sides met with a cold attitude. One day, Naomi yelled at Jules.

"I've been depressed for days, and you don't even know," she said.

Jules was silent for a while. He had no idea why they were talking on the phone when even messaging was quite difficult. He was angry at her for getting them into this situation.

"I've been feeling alone for months," he replied. Naomi was silent for a while in the response. At that moment, Jules continued to scribble with a red pen on his old drawings.

Suddenly the phone hung up. Jules had heard Naomi crying before she hung up. He was surprised that he didn't feel anything in this situation. There was a numbness inside him. He continued to scribble indifferently.

When he turned the page, he found a note paper that was stuck in between. It was a handwritten letter he had written to Naomi years ago:

*Today I saw a red car. It had the perfect shade that you would love.*

*Wish you were with me.*

*Love you forever,*

*Jules*

It was handwritten with a red pen. He began to turn the pages of his notebook backward one by one. He had filled the world of drawing with shades of green and blue, supported by gray, black, and negative white areas.

In addition to his favorite colors, there were also occasional touches of red on these pages.

When there were no red, shades of purple, pink, and dark brown would show themselves. He remembered that these colors reflected Naomi's drawing world. When he first saw it, he thought what an interesting color choice it was. Over time, he began to admire how Naomi combined these difficult colors with each other.

What he saw reminded him of other things. He opened the music app on his phone and scrolled down to his old playlists. He found the playlists he had created for Naomi years ago and examined them one by one. There were songs on the list that he hadn't listened to in a long time.

One of the songs reminded him of the live streams he used to do with Naomi. They would open live broadcasts that nobody watched and review TV series, movies, and games. Each time, they would share different approaches to the subject they examined and eventually start arguing but in a playful way.

The memories were enough to pull Jules out of the red scribbling trance he had been in for the past few days. He started looking out of the window of the café he was sitting in, watching the people on the street. He wanted to dispel the clouds in his mind.

He thought he hadn't read a book in a while. Months ago, Naomi had recommended a book to him. She said she had read it in her childhood and sometimes returned to it. It was one of her favorite books.

Jules found the book and downloaded it onto his e-reader to start reading. The main character of the book was a little boy named Ronald. He lived with his family in the Snow World. His father cleared snow in the town they lived in. Because in this world, winter lasted nine months and caused meters of snow in the town.

His father's biggest enemy was the owner of the other snow removal job. This person had a daughter named Bonnie, who was the same age as Ronald. Although Ronald and Bonnie were raised to hate each other, their curiosity brought them together and caused them to form a secret friendship.

Through their friendship, they started exploring the magical world of meters-deep snow in the Snow World. Jules had been reading the book for hours. It was already dark when he turned the last page, and the cafe workers kindly informed him that the cafe would be closing soon.

As he walked home, he thought about the Snow World. When he was young, he used to live in his imagination and go on adventures like Ronald. That's why he started drawing at a very young age. While other kids tried to draw what they saw around them or what they were told to draw, Jules would draw what he imagined.

For months, he would imagine himself in a space adventure and draw all the details of that world. Then, inspired by something else he saw, he would reshape the adventure he had embarked on. Adventures in magical worlds never ended for him.

No matter what he experienced in his life, he was able to remain himself by holding onto his imagination. When his friend asked him, "What is your greatest achievement in life?" he replied, "Not losing the child inside me and holding onto my imaginary world."

When he met Naomi, he noticed this about her too. He saw a part of himself in her. They both applied to a technical drawing exhibition without fully understanding the subject and were accepted. The warm paintings that shone among the sharp, cold, and mechanical drawings in the exhibition were only their paintings. They met and joked around with the exhibition owners and other artists throughout the exhibition.

In the following days, they continued to meet outside of the exhibition. Jules always remembered these days as the most beautiful days of his life. However, Naomi had to return to her country after those wonderful days.

He had never been able to form such a strong bond with anyone in his life. That's why he decided to have a long-distance relationship. There had never been any problems between them for the first six years. However, in the last year, everything had fallen off the cliff, starting with the arguments about their drawing worlds and how different they were.

Jules entered his house and slumped onto the couch. He thought about Ronald and Bonnie.

They were very different. But they were the same.

Suddenly, he felt filled with anxiety as if he was looking at everything in his life from the wrong angle. He evaluated the happiest moments in his life, most of which he was able to share with Naomi. He thought about his first exhibition. After the exhibition, he quickly made a drawing in his notebook and sent her a message.

He got up from the couch and went to his bookshelf. He searched for the notebook in which he had made that drawing. He quickly flipped through the pages and found the draft he was looking for.

He had drawn a dragon embracing a phoenix. The two mystical creatures represented Naomi and Jules. After his first exhibition, he sent this drawing to Naomi and told her how much he wanted to hug her at that moment.

A year later, Naomi also sent him a drawing after her first exhibition. This drawing seemed very different to Jules that day. He searched for that drawing on his phone.

Two mechanical robots, created in steam technology style, were embracing each other. They symbolized Jules and Naomi. When Naomi sent this drawing to Jules, she told him how much she wanted to hug him.

Jules sat back on the couch with both drawings in front of him. He felt as if the knot inside him had been untied.

Tears filled his eyes, and he began to cry.

He took out his notebook and red pen from his bag and started writing a message on a blank page.

*Thank you for the book recommendation.*

*I have always been searching for my Bonnie throughout my life. When I'm with you, I feel at home. It's like I'm in the Snow World because I know you will always be there.*

*Love you forever,*

*Jules*
