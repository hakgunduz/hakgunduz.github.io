title: "Carrot Mountain"
date: 2023-02-05 19:56:02 +0000
---

When the little girl asked for a carrot cake, she couldn't have guessed that the only chef in the mountain village hated making cakes.

The name of the mountain village, which had about twenty-five households, was Taneler. The only restaurant in this village was run by the only Chef in the mountain village. In fact, it wouldn't even be called a restaurant.

The hall at the entrance of a cottage house was turned into a restaurant with three or five tables. Meals were cooked in the kitchen of the house and served in the living room at the entrance.

One morning, a twelve-year-old girl named Deniz stood in the middle of the restaurant and asked the chef for a carrot cake. The chef burst into laughter as soon as he heard this. After serving the spinach and beans dish to his guests, he went inside grumbling.

The girl's mother was the chef’s assistant. Normally she was supposed to serve the dish, but she was taken aback when she heard her daughter's momentary request. She couldn't move for a moment because she was afraid of the chef.

Deniz pulled her mother aside. The customers in the restaurant were pretending to continue their meals. However, they were following what was going on with great interest.

“Mom, are we here for this man?” ' asked Deniz.

“Your mother has called a lot, my daughter,” her mother said. “I searched a lot to find someone else, to stay in town. But no one was willing to help. Nobody hired me.”

“But he doesn't teach you anything,” Deniz said.

“Yes, but he's an excellent chef,” her mother said. She always treated her like an adult.

Together they were watching the chef through the doorway. He was skillfully frying the vegetables in the big frying pan in his hand, while he was looking for something with the other hand. While doing all this, he never stopped grumbling.

"Yasemin!" shouted the chef. “We are out of parsley, I've been cooking with cilantro for an hour. Go and get parsley," he said.

Hearing this, the customers suddenly started talking among themselves. “Did he use cilantro? So that's why it's so beautiful!” “Yes, it is beautiful!” “This is the first time I've heard of this dish, what's its name?”

“Actually, it doesn't have a name,” said Yasemin. "Here's a new recipe inspired by Ribollita." Then they went out with Deniz to buy parsley.

In the following days, Yasemin started to try various carrot cake recipes for her daughter. For this, she got permission from the chef to use the restaurant's oven. The chef bluntly avoided paying attention. However, he secretly and curiously followed all of Yasemin's recipe attempts.

On her first try, the strained yogurt from Aunt Fatima prevented the cake from rising. The second time, she used the yogurt she bought from the market, but this time she cut her hand while grating the carrots.

The chef was finding and tasting the unsuccessful cake that Yasemin had set aside every time she tried it.

The cake on the first try was too liquid. The second time the carrots were not cooked. The cinnamon of the third was very dominant. The fourth was not sweet at all. The fifth one's walnuts were mouth-wrenching… And so it went on.

The Chef, who hated baking cakes, finally couldn't stand it. On Yasemin's fourteenth attempt, he saw her pouring the sugar bowl into the cake mix and shouted.

"Abomination!" the chef said. “That’s too much sugar for the cake?”

Soon after, he pretended not to care at all and disappeared. The days passed like this. As the guests came and went, they followed the carrot cake process with interest.

Yasemin dropped what she was holding and fell to the ground in defeat. "Why am I trying so hard?" she asked herself. “She should just eat one of those,” she said, looking at the failed cake attempts.

The chef poked his head through the door. “Do you consider these worthy of your daughter?”

"I don't understand what I'm doing wrong," said Yasemin. "Can you help me?"

“Ha! Baking is not elegant at all. Very mechanical!” cried the chef. According to Yasemin, this tone was now the chef’s normal tone.

“Even a monkey can do it,” said the chef. “You follow the recipe exactly. There's no accident. Not elegant at all!”

He turned his back. He was putting the food he had prepared on the plate.

“None of the recipes are good anyways…” said Yasemin. She was talking more to herself. “I wish we could create our own recipe.”

The chief abruptly quit his job and stood up. His back was still turned to Yasemin.

“We follow the recipe, but the ingredients we use are not the same,” continued Yasemin.

“What makes carrot cake, carrot cake?” the chef asked.

“Carrots, of course. Flour, walnuts, cinnamon maybe. The frosting. That's probably it?" asked Jasmine.

“We will make our own recipe,” said the chef.

"How?" said Yasemin.

“Simple…” the chef insisted. He had already begun to rummage through the kitchen cabinets.

Yasemin was excited. “We can make the perfect carrot cake,” she said.

“We can make the carrot cake that Deniz will love,” said the chef.

They immediately set about making a cake. However, the chef deviated so much from the recipe that the resulting mixture didn't look like carrot cake at all.

Yasemin watched the chef with great admiration for a while. The chef checked the recipe a few times but never followed it. He found it had a lot of sugar. He added starch and extra baking powder to the cake mix according to his taste. He got bored while grating the carrots and handed the job over to Yasemin.

When all things were finished, they put the cake in the oven. Although Yasemin was excited, she thought that something was wrong. As a matter of fact, when the cake came out of the oven, she understood why she felt that way. The chef deviated so much from the recipe that the resulting cake experiment was even worse than Yasemin's previous attempts.

Yasemin thought for a while. What made the chef so good at cooking was his ability to improvise with snap decisions. However, this made the job difficult while baking cakes.

“Not elegant at all!” shouted the chef. He was so bored. “If I have to follow the recipe exactly, then what is my contribution?!”

“We played with so many variables at once,” said Yasemin. She was suddenly able to think in a very organized way.

“Let's take the recipe we find the best. And let's experiment by changing the variables in it one by one,” she said. She spoke without thinking. She was very scared when she realized what she had done. She was ready for the chef to yell at her.

However, she felt that he had made up his mind too because without saying a word, the chef was getting ready to make the cake again.

As the days passed, they began to perfect their cake recipes. They proceeded with small decisions on many issues, from the ratio of sugar and which sugar to use, to how to dilute Aunt Fatima's yogurt.

The whole village was watching what was going on. Cake trials had begun to be distributed throughout the village. Village residents ate more carrot cake than they had ever eaten in their lives. Only Deniz was not very fond of these cakes. She was picking up a fork, tasting it once, and then leaving the rest.

After trial after trial, they felt they were getting very close. However, they could not take the final step. Their recipes were one step away from perfection. For three days they couldn't figure out what to do for that final step.

The sugar ratio, the consistency of the mixture they spread on the cake, the texture of the cakes, the feel of the carrot flavor… Everything was in perfect balance. However, there was something wrong.

One morning, while the chef was clearing out the kitchen, he saw a packet of cinnamon in the trash. At that moment, an idea occurred to him. He woke Yasemin early and invited her to the restaurant.

When Yasemin came to the restaurant, she found herself in a completely clean kitchen. On the kitchen island was a slice of carrot cake. She quickly grabbed the fork and tasted it. She had tears in his eyes.

The chef told her how he didn't buy the cinnamon ready and how he broke the cinnamon stick with a knife. The last missing piece was found.

At noon the same day, Deniz tasted the carrot cake. On the one hand, she was playing with the rabbit toy in her hand, and on the other hand, she was eating the cake. All the village people gathered in the hall of the restaurant, watching Deniz.

Chef and Yasemin followed Deniz with the same attention.

Deniz left the toy in her hand after she finished her cake. She took her plate to the kitchen.

“Mom, can I have another slice?” she said.

At the same time, celebrations began among the villagers. They all started clapping together. Yasemin took the plate from Deniz's hand and put another slice of carrot cake.

The chef was in a corner of the kitchen, his back turned, quietly tidying up. When the noise of the voice from inside subsided a little, he came to Yasemin.

“I am not a teacher,” said the chef. “I don't know how to teach what I'm doing. But I will try."

“Thanks, Chief Necmi,” said Jasmine.

Chef Necmi invited Yasemin to the stove where he usually spends his time. He lit the fire and placed the pan on it.

“I did not receive any training. So I will tell you as I know. From the beginning…"

Yasemin shook her head excitedly.

“Cooking is a sensory experience. First of all, you should have a very good understanding of this.” He pointed at the stove.

“You need to follow the food’s reaction to the fire.”
